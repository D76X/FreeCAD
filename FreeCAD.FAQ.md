# How do I zoom in and out in FreeCAD 1.1?

To zoom in and out in FreeCAD 1.1, use your mouse wheel or the keyboard shortcuts depending on your active navigation style (the default is CAD or Blender navigation): [1] (https://wiki.freecad.org/Manual:Navigating_in_the_3D_View)

Standard Mouse Wheel Zoom

• Zoom In: Scroll the mouse wheel forward (up).
• Zoom Out: Scroll the mouse wheel backward (down). [1] (https://cadin360.com/blog/freecad/how-to-zoom-in-and-zoom-out-in-freecad/)

Alternative Navigation Styles

If you use a different navigation style (such as Maya, Touchpad, or Inventor), the zoom controls vary: [1] (https://wiki.freecad.org/Manual:Navigating_in_the_3D_View)
• CAD (Default): Scroll the mouse wheel, or use Ctrl + Shift + right-click and move the pointer (depending on mode).
• Blender: Scroll the mouse wheel.
• Maya-Gesture: Use the mouse wheel or hold Alt + right-click and drag.
• Touchpad: Hold Ctrl + Shift and move the pointer.
• Tip: You can check or change your navigation style by right-clicking the navigation name in the bottom-right corner of the FreeCAD window or by going to Edit > Preferences > Display > Navigation. [1] (https://wiki.freecad.org/Mouse_navigation), [2] (https://wiki.freecad.org/Mouse_navigation/sv), [3] (https://wiki.freecad.org/Mouse_navigation/uk), [4] (https://wiki.freecad.org/Template:Touchpad_Navigation), [5] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3aiD7CsU-4o), [6] (https://www.reddit.com/r/FreeCAD/comments/sd5cdt/change_zoom_focus_behaviour/), [7] (https://wiki.freecad.org/Manual:Navigating_in_the_3D_View)

Keyboard Shortcuts & Quick Tools

• Page Up / Page Down: Use PgUp to zoom in and PgDn to zoom out quickly. [1] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3aiD7CsU-4o)
• Zoom to Fit ("Fit All"): Click the Fit All button on the toolbar (or press V, F) to reset the view so your entire model fits on the screen. [1] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=85HijsjgsFw), [2] (https://cadin360.com/blog/freecad/how-to-zoom-in-and-zoom-out-in-freecad/)
• Zoom at Cursor: You can toggle whether FreeCAD zooms toward the center of the screen or directly toward your mouse pointer under Edit > Preferences > Viewer > 3D viewer by checking the Zoom at cursor option. [1] (https://forum.freecad.org/viewtopic.php?t=10701), [2] (https://forum.freecad.org/viewtopic.php?t=10821)
